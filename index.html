The Very Boring-Looking Perfect Pitch Test™

Why does this website exist?

I made this website for two reasons:
  1. I have to make a flavourless website for a Fraser Hack Club workshop and this was the only decent idea that came to mind.
  2. I have perfect pitch, but I am very slow at identifying notes and need something to train my pitch detection.

So because of this, I thought to myself, why not make a quiz to test if you can identify the note played correctly? So here's a test to find out if you have perfect pitch, and/or if you're good at having perfect pitch.

Please note that this test is intended to only be taken once. The questions are not randomized. (also no cheating please be honest about your score if you tell your friends about your result)

This is a 10 question quiz that will progressively get harder as the quiz continues.
